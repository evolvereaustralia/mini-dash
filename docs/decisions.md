# Decisions

One line of reasoning per decision, dated. Newest at the bottom. Hardware and architecture decisions made in chat before the repo existed are summarised in CLAUDE.md; this file starts at the repo.

- 2026-10-14: Hardware locked (4.3C, NEO-M9N SMA, ISM330DHCX, VEML7700, Qwiic). Reasons in CLAUDE.md section 2.
- 2026-10-23: OBD input (vLinker BM+, BLE, BMW UDS) brought into the first release instead of a later phase; bring-up stays staged because the dongle has not arrived.
- 2026-10-24: Dovetail flipped: rail on the shell, groove in the plate. A dovetail groove in a face that is vertical in print has a lip that starts in mid-air and cannot print; a rail with its wide end on the bed can.
- 2026-10-24: Rail leans back by RAIL_LEAN_DEG = 10 (nominal dash tilt) so the plate groove is vertical in print; the shell no longer changes when the tilt is measured, the plate absorbs it.
- 2026-10-24: Printability is checked layer by layer (check_overhangs.py, 45-degree step) before any STL is released; PrusaSlicer's warnings had been catching what a face-normal check missed.
- 2026-10-24: GPS board on 3 mm bosses, components toward the display, so its edge-mount SMA jack and plug clear the display PCB back by 1 mm. IMU moved to x = 26 to clear a connector row on the display back. Clearances taken from Waveshare's STEP, not the bare PCB outline.
- 2026-10-24: Second notch through the visor floor for the light sensor cable; the coax notch is under the antenna.
- 2026-10-24: Two Qwiic-to-header cables into the one screw terminal (IMU chain and light sensor) because the light sensor is out of 50 mm reach and the GPS board's second port faces the wall.
- 2026-10-24: M2 x 4 heat-set inserts and M2 x 6 screws for all sensor mounts: the CE10192 kit has no M2.5 and M3 inserts do not fit a 6 mm boss.
- 2026-10-24: USB-C opening is a notch from the shell's seam edge rather than a window, removing a 16 mm bridge.
- 2026-10-24: Plate adhesive is genuine 3M VHB 5952, not a general-purpose mounting tape: the F56 dash top is a grained low-surface-energy plastic (electrical tape would not stick), Gorilla's data sheet excludes polypropylene, and 5952 is the grade phone-mount makers use on dashes. Promoter only if a hidden test patch fails, because promoter makes removal leave a mark.
- 2026-10-24: Firmware toolchain is PlatformIO on the pioarduino platform release 55.03.311 (Arduino core 3.3.11, the version Waveshare's ci.json pins); the official PlatformIO espressif32 platform stopped at Arduino core 2.x.
- 2026-10-24: PlatformIO runs from a virtualenv on Homebrew python@3.12 (`~/.platformio/penv`, `pio` symlinked into /opt/homebrew/bin) because the pioarduino build scripts refuse Python 3.15, which is what Homebrew's own platformio formula ships.
- 2026-10-24: Waveshare's Arduino io_extension driver was ported onto Wire and the one I2cBus mutex instead of ESP-IDF's i2c_master API, so the SparkFun and Adafruit sensor libraries (which use Wire) and the board controller share one bus driver and one lock.
- 2026-10-24: The IO expander's PWM register is inverted (0 = brightest, Waveshare caps the dim end at 95 percent so the panel never blanks). IoExpander::setBacklightPercent takes human brightness and hides the inversion.
- 2026-10-24: Expected I2C map gains 0x40 (ES7210 microphone ADC, Waveshare es7210_adc.h 0x80 in 8-bit form) and confirms 0x18 for the ES8311 codec (es8311_codec.h 0x30 in 8-bit form). Both are on the bus whether we use audio or not, so the scan must expect them.
- 2026-10-24: Phase 3a drives the RGB panel with esp_lcd_panel_rgb directly, copied from Waveshare's rgb_lcd_port (IDF 5.5 renames psram_trans_align to dma_burst_size). Whether phase 3b uses ESP32_Display_Panel as CLAUDE.md section 5 says, or Waveshare's own esp_lcd_touch GT911 port, is still to decide.
- 2026-10-24: UI merged from the v2 brief and the telemetry design handoff (docs/design/handoff): six tabs Drive, Trip, Dynamics, Energy, Battery, Charge from the handoff replace Dash, EV, G detail, Timers, Trip, Settings; swipe stays as a second way to change tab; Settings by header tap. The merged prototype docs/design/mini-dash-ui.html is the visual reference.
- 2026-10-24: The handoff's demo tag in the header becomes the brief's status cluster (GPS, OBD, day or night, clock); its marketing taglines go, the space carries live data.
- 2026-10-24: 100-0 braking (time, with distance) is a Trip headline tile; max braking G moves to Journey totals and the Dynamics peaks row, because the braking timer was a committed brief feature and the G peak is still shown twice.
- 2026-10-24: The brief's EV page items find homes: torque, pedal and motor temperatures on Dynamics; current, cell voltages, 12 V on Battery; range and 12 V on Drive.
- 2026-10-24: Power bar centred on zero with an asymmetric scale (60 kW regen left in cyan, 150 kW draw right in lime), not the handoff's left-anchored segments, so regen stays visible and direction does not rely on colour.
- 2026-10-24: Energy chart plots signed battery kW over 10 minutes, not rolling Wh/km, which is undefined at low speed; Wh/km is the trip figure and only shown instantaneously above 15 km/h.
- 2026-10-24: Type scale raised for the 0.117 mm pixel: speed 88 px, tiles 40, footer values 26, labels 16, parked-only text 14. Only numerals of 40 px and up are glanceable at about 75 cm.
- 2026-10-24: Dark palette always; night mode is backlight PWM through the expander, driven by the VEML7700 (the handoff assumed no light sensor). The light palette is a later experiment for sun on the windscreen.
- 2026-10-24: Splash at power-on: typographic MINI / COOPER SE with a bottom status line, on within 300 ms, over to Drive after 1.5 to 3 s. Doubles as the acquiring / connecting state. Logan may supply artwork to replace the wordmark.
- 2026-10-24: Trip history, event traces and charge sessions go on a LittleFS partition on the internal flash (9 MB already reserved by the partition scheme), bounded to the last 60 trips; all-time records and settings stay in NVS; still nothing on the TF card.
- 2026-10-24: Font Inter with tabular digits converted for LVGL, because Montserrat lacks the dashes, dots, arrows and degree sign the design uses and its digits are not tabular.
- 2026-10-24: A labelled demo replay mode is built first in phase 3b, isolated from records, so the UI can be developed and reviewed on the bench without driving.
- 2026-10-24: The device is switched off whenever the car is parked or charging (powered only from the car's USB-C, no battery, no sleep mode). So a trip is one power cycle, checkpointed every 30 s, and the Charge tab is dropped: five tabs of 160 px, Drive, Trip, Dynamics, Energy, Battery.
- 2026-10-24: Splash artwork is the MINI wings emblem Logan supplied, lifted off its white background by a flood fill from the corners (the emblem has a continuous dark outline, so the white ring inside survives), stored in docs/design/assets at 420 px wide with transparency.
- 2026-10-24: Display pipeline: LVGL partial rendering into a 40-row internal-RAM buffer, chunks copied into the back PSRAM framebuffer, swap on the last chunk, dirty rows synced to the new back buffer. Direct mode into PSRAM cost about 150 ms a frame of per-pixel blending; this costs about 55 ms. No tearing either way.
- 2026-10-24: The G plot keeps only the static layer and the density cloud on a canvas (redrawn at most once a second); trail, vector, dot and peak markers are LVGL objects, so a tick invalidates small areas rather than the whole plot.
- 2026-10-24: Labels whose text changes every tick are fixed-width and right-aligned instead of re-aligned each tick; re-aligning invalidated them every frame even when nothing changed.
- 2026-10-24: LVGL's hot draw code goes in IRAM (LV_ATTRIBUTE_FAST_MEM = IRAM_ATTR); internal heap after boot is about 94 KB, watched.
- 2026-10-24: Inter's tabular digits are made the default digits by rewriting the font's cmap through the tnum GSUB lookups with fontTools, because lv_font_conv does not apply OpenType features.
- 2026-10-24: The UI tick is 10 Hz; frames follow the tick. The handoff's 30 fps target waits on cheaper rendering of anti-aliased text and lines.
- 2026-10-24: Demo replay is on by default while phase 3b is under way (bench use); it is labelled DEMO in the header and resets the trip when switched.
- 2026-10-24: UI text is positioned by baseline from the font metrics, not by box top. The converted digit-only faces have no leading, so box-top placement collided the speed and SOC figures with their captions (Logan's photo); a baseline grid per page also fixes the uneven alignment between mixed sizes.
- 2026-10-24: Settings gets a gear icon at the right of the header (Montserrat's built-in symbol) because the header tap was undiscoverable; the whole header remains the tap target.
- 2026-10-24: Hardware behaviour is only logged as a pass when a person or an instrument saw it; the touch row had been marked from read statistics alone and was wrong.
- 2026-10-24: GPS I2C transactions are 128 bytes (the ESP32 Wire buffer) and the receiver's UBX-only, nav-rate and autoPVT settings are checked by their acknowledgements and retried; the 80 to 108 ms bus holds went away with this.
- 2026-10-24: The I2C mutex stays non-recursive and now rejects and logs a nested take from its own holder, because a nested take silently cost 50 ms per GPS fix and halved the IMU rate.
- 2026-10-24: Storage layout: NVS for settings, mounting reference, lifetime records and the trip id counter; LittleFS on the "ffat" partition for /trips/<id>.trp (last 60), /events/<id>-<rec>.evt (12 s at 10 Hz, 10 bytes a sample) and /active.trp (checkpoint every 30 s). Files are written to a temporary name and renamed so a power cut leaves the old or the new file, never a torn one.
- 2026-10-24: Demo trips are never stored and never touch today or lifetime records (the engine runs isolated while the demo is on); their event traces are deleted when the demo trip ends.
- 2026-10-24: Session record comparisons are against the last stored trip; a first-ever record says so. Today is seeded from today's stored trips at trip start, lifetime from NVS.
- 2026-10-24: Wall clock from the board's PCF85063A RTC, set from GPS once per boot; UTC stored, Australia/Melbourne with the DST rule for display and day grouping.
- 2026-10-24: Flash is written only while the car is still (speed under 2 km/h for 2 s), at trip end, or when a checkpoint is 10 min overdue. A flash write blocks PSRAM reads and the panel then repeats stale ten-line strips; with a static picture the glitch is invisible. Settings writes stay immediate because they follow a tap on a static page.
- 2026-10-24: Deferring flash writes to still moments is kept but is not sufficient: the panel repeats one band everywhere while the refill is stalled, so any erase is visible. The fix is to build the framework with code and read-only data executing from PSRAM and the RGB refill interrupt IRAM-safe (pioarduino custom_sdkconfig, env minidash-xip); the prebuilt core lacks both.
- 2026-10-24: File writes no longer go through a temporary file and rename; LittleFS updates a file atomically on close, and each extra step cost erases.
- 2026-10-24: On Drive, battery power takes the large right-hand slot and state of charge the smaller left one, because power changes every second and SOC barely moves (Logan).
- 2026-10-24: Drive's pack line shows the HV pack's current temperature and voltage, not the 12 V battery, which barely moves and matters only when failing; it stays on the Battery page (Logan).
- 2026-10-24: The firmware builds on an Arduino core rebuilt from ESP-IDF with code and read-only data executing from PSRAM and the RGB refill interrupt IRAM-safe (env minidash-xip, the default). Measured: a flash write stalls PSRAM access for 1.5 to 1.8 ms instead of 19 to 20 ms, so writes are immediate on this build. Env minidash (prebuilt core) stays as a fallback with the standstill deferral; MINIDASH_XIP selects the behaviour.
- 2026-10-24: Stay on one env. The hybrid build swaps the prebuilt libraries package in place, and each switch between minidash-xip and minidash or bringup costs a three-minute rebuild.
- 2026-10-24: pioarduino's tool-scons version check is patched per machine in the platform's platform.json (firmware/README.md). A workaround inside the project (pinning the package, pre-importing SCons modules) could not work because the platform deletes the tool before any project script runs.
- 2026-10-24: Event files whose trip has no trip file are removed at boot (demo trips, trips lost before their first checkpoint), so demo traces do not accumulate on the partition.
- 2026-10-25: Attitude is a quaternion propagated from the gyro at 208 Hz (Mahony form), pulled toward the accelerometer only when the specific force is within 3 percent of 1 g, gyro rates under 10 deg/s and the GPS speed steady (gain 0.1 per second, a 10 s time constant), fully at a standstill (gain 1), and fast for the first 3 s after power-on, the car being parked then. The gyro bias is the low-passed gyro at standstill (2 s time constant for the first 10 s, then 20 s) plus the integral of the gravity error in cruise.
- 2026-10-25: The accelerometer's zero-g offset is not estimated. At rest the filter absorbs it into the up direction; the residual while moving is the offset times the change of pitch, well under 1 mg.
- 2026-10-25: The stored mounting reference only seeds the attitude filter at boot so the G display is right from the first sample. Vehicle-frame G comes from a nominal forward axis in board_config.h (IMU_FORWARD_SENSOR) projected onto the horizontal plane of the current up; the mount's pitch and roll never need calibrating.
- 2026-10-25: Displayed speed is GPS fused with the integrated longitudinal g: the IMU carries the estimate, each GPS sample corrects it (gain 0.35) against the estimate 100 ms earlier, the receiver's latency as a constant until a drive refines it. At standstill the speed is pinned to zero and the longitudinal residual learnt; the IMU ends the standstill at 0.05 g before the GPS notices. The timers never see the fused value: the engine takes the raw GPS sample separately.
- 2026-10-25: The attitude and fusion maths are plain C++ tested natively on the Mac (firmware/test/native/run.sh) against a synthetic drive with bounds; the bench can only prove the rest case.
- 2026-10-25: The GPS receiver's occasional refused read (clock stretched past the chip's 26 ms timeout) is counted, not fixed.
- 2026-10-25: The attitude filter trusts the accelerometer when its magnitude is within 3 percent of the magnitude learnt at rest, not of 1 g: the ISM330DHCX reads 1.02 to 1.05 g at rest depending on orientation (zero-g offsets and scale error), and a gate on 1 g silently disabled the gravity correction on the bench.
- 2026-10-25: Accelerometer offsets are measured once per sensor with a sphere fit over ten orientations (docs/calibration.md) and every raw sample is corrected before the attitude filter sees it. An offset absorbed into the up direction is harmless at rest but swings with yaw; this unit had 25 mg on one axis, 1.5 degrees.
- 2026-10-25: Stillness for the calibration is judged from the accelerometer window (30 mg spread over 0.3 s), not the gyro: a hand-held board never gets the gyro under a few deg/s, and a sliding window means a twitch costs 0.3 s rather than the position.
- 2026-10-25: Settings blob version 2 (accelerometer calibration added); a version change resets stored settings to defaults once.
- 2026-11-05: Draft 2.6 shell. Both floor notches moved BEHIND the bezel pocket wall (z -22 to -13.5): draft 2.5 had them in front of it, where the 13 mm bezel wall blocks the tray, so no cable could reach the visor (found by Logan on the printed shell).
- 2026-11-05: GPS board moved to the right half and rotated 180 degrees (SMA jack pointing left) because the antenna lead has a 30 mm stiff section next to the plug: plug plus stiff lead need 50 mm dead straight from the jack, which now runs along y = 4 under bare PCB. Coax notch widened to 10 mm so plug and stiff section can be fed through at an angle; the left antenna rib stops at z -3 so the lead can leave the pocket at its front corner. IMU moved to the upper left, above that lane.
- 2026-11-05: Display press bosses replaced by four 5 mm pads on bare PCB, placed from Waveshare's STEP. The old bosses at the PCB holes seated on the DC terminal and corner standoffs and let the display sit 2 mm off-centre in the shell; the bezel pocket centres the glass, the pads only press.
- 2026-11-05: Light sensor held by M2 x 12 screws down through counterbored holes in the visor roof with nuts under the board, not inserts: there is no iron access to a boss hanging inside the visor, and a screw head on the roof looks better than a nut (Logan's call).
- 2026-11-05: GPS bosses 2 mm with the kit's M2 x 3 inserts (was 3 mm, M2 x 4) to buy clearance for the plug under the display back. SMA knockout is now a recess leaving a 0.4 mm skin, drilled only if the external puck is ever needed.
- 2026-11-08: Datum error found from Logan's photos of the 2.6 shell with only the display fitted (bezel 2 mm proud): the model took device z = 0 as the glass front, but the glass sits behind the 2 mm bezel plate, so every display-back part was 2 mm deeper than modelled and the press pads stopped 2 mm short. Fixed with PCB_BACK_Z = -(BEZ_T + STACK) = -12.2.
- 2026-11-08: Draft 2.7 (same day, withdrawn): tried to keep the 25 mm shell by running the SMA plug beside the display board; it assumed the tray was 97 mm tall inside when it is 80.3, so there is no such band. Never released.
- 2026-11-08: Draft 2.8: shell deepened by the same 2 mm (DEPTH 27) so the 2.6 layout, already checked against the display back, is unchanged relative to it; the 7.9 mm SMA plug keeps 1.5 mm under the PCB. Device back lands 12 mm from the windscreen edge instead of 14. Pad test coupon added so the datum is proven before the next shell print.
- 2026-11-08: GPS board screws are M2 x 4 (a 6 mm screw bottoms in the 3.5 mm hole); IMU stays on M2 x 6.
- 2026-11-08: Pad coupon printed by Logan: three pads reached the PCB, the fourth at (36, -21) sat on the GT911 chip on the touch flex, which folds behind the PCB and is not in Waveshare's STEP. Pad moved to (44.5, -20); the flex footprint is now in the drawings' part list.
- 2026-11-09: Logan's square-on back photo shows the STEP omits the touch flex and chip, the speaker, the SPK and battery connectors, the coin cell holder, the 6-pin JST and a PCB notch, and that its 'DC terminal' is the BOOT/RESET pair. Two coupon prints had landed STEP-placed pads on parts. Rule from now: place nothing from the STEP alone; the photo (in hardware/datasheets) is the reference.
- 2026-11-09: Draft 2.9. Press pads moved onto the four corner screw heads (certain positions, load into the panel frame). DEPTH 27 -> 31 so the plug lane keeps 5.5 mm under the PCB and clears the optocouplers and the speaker's edge. Coax notch replaced by a 6 mm slot open to the visor front: the plug and 50 mm rigid section cannot be threaded through any hole in the top wall, so the flexible lead is laid in from the front.
- 2026-11-09: Coupon with pads on the screw heads seated the glass perfectly. Logan's measurements (speaker flush, heads 1 mm, SPK connector 2 mm from the head, nothing over 5 mm on the lane) close the open clearances. GPS corner to (48, 27) and IMU to (-30, 20) on 2.5 mm bosses so neither board meets a press pad and the IMU plug clears the SPK connector; display_back_parts.py and check_layout.py added so every layout is checked against the photo-derived parts, not the STEP.
- 2026-11-24: The braking record is 60-0, not 100-0 (Logan): a stop from 100 km/h is not something his roads allow, a stop from 60 happens on every drive. Timed from the downward 60 km/h crossing under braking to under 1 km/h, with distance. Trip files written before the change keep everything but their braking record; a stored lifetime one is dropped once.
- 2026-11-24: OBD runs on the Arduino core's own BLE library, which the core builds on the NimBLE host: the brief's "NimBLE, not Bluedroid" with no extra dependency.
- 2026-11-24: ECU addresses and every identifier come from OVMS vehicle_minise and OBDb MINI-Cooper, cited per line in obd_pids.h; where only one source has a value (DSC wheel speeds) the client tries it and falls back to the one both have.
- 2026-11-24: Displayed SOC is the battery ECU's display SOC, unscaled. The brief's plan to scale it between the reported maximum and minimum was tried first and was wrong for this car: 50.7 from the ECU, 51 on the dash, 42 after scaling (Logan read the dash). The SOC bar is a plain 0 to 100.
- 2026-11-24: The poll scheduler visits one ECU at a time and asks for everything due there, because each change of ECU costs three adapter commands; rate is measured, not assumed.
- 2026-11-24: LVGL's 96 KB object pool moved from an internal static array to PSRAM. The Bluetooth stack needs 72 KB of internal RAM and had 28 KB; the draw buffer and draw code, which set the frame time, stay internal. Cost measured with the demo: UI handler 30 to 39 ms average against 23 to 33.
- 2026-11-24: The paired dongle and the OBD on/off switch live in their own NVS keys, not in the settings blob, so pairing can never reset the calibration. A stored dongle is the only one the client connects to; Forget dongle in Settings lets the next scan take any vLinker.
- 2026-11-24: OBD schedule after measuring in the car: battery current and wheel speeds as fast as the link allows (1.9 Hz each), pack voltage twice a second, torque and pedal about every 2 s, because only power and speed are read while driving and every change of ECU costs about 50 ms. The adapter is told how many frames to expect so it does not wait out its timeout, and one receive filter covers all ECUs.
- 2026-11-24: Independent review of commit dc9e9b4 accepted in full: 42 findings and an appendix, each checked against the source and found correct. docs/review-findings.md is the ledger; fixes are on branch review-fixes, built and tested natively, not yet run on the device.
- 2026-11-24: A 60-0 braking record is a continuous braking stop (Logan): at least 0.1 g throughout from the 60 km/h crossing to standstill, coasting over 2 s in total or any rise in speed cancels. A coast down to a stop is not a record. To be implemented with the timer rework after the first logged drive.
- 2026-11-24: Trips under 50 m are discarded (Logan): a power-on in the garage leaves no trip. Nothing a trip does reaches Today or the lifetime records until it has covered 50 m, so a discarded trip cannot leave a record behind.
- 2026-11-24: Pairing a new dongle is a deliberate act (Logan): only after a Pair tap in Settings while parked, and stored only once the car has answered through it. The first dongle used to be whichever "vLinker" had the strongest signal.
- 2026-11-24: A drive that crosses midnight belongs to the day it started (Logan). A clock that arrives after the trip began dates the trip from its elapsed time; the trip is not restarted.
- 2026-11-24: One task writes trips, records, traces and settings: the Arduino loop task (app/persist.*). Three tasks used to write, and the engine was read while it changed. Everything else leaves a request.
- 2026-11-24: Files are replaced by writing a second file, syncing, reading it back and renaming it over the old one. The LittleFS port in this build syncs a file as soon as it is opened for writing, so opening the target itself committed an empty file first.
- 2026-11-24: A mount failure never formats a partition that has held data; serial KK is the one deliberate format. Losing a boot's storage is better than losing sixty trips.
- 2026-11-24: Turn gate on the attitude filter: speed times yaw rate over 0.05 g holds the accelerometer off, released after 0.5 s under 0.03 g. The reviewer's starting values; tune from the first logged drive.
- 2026-11-24: Gyro bias is learnt only on GPS evidence of a standstill. Without a fix a quiet IMU could be a long curve; the cost is that the bias is not learnt before the first fix, which normally comes within seconds.
- 2026-11-24: The forward axis is a stored setting with a run-time guard, not a constant: +Y on the bench, -Z in the enclosure. A wrong setting shows dashes instead of plausible nonsense.
- 2026-11-24: Capture mode is a stored run-time switch, on by default, not a second build environment: one firmware to flash, no second framework rebuild, and the device cannot store records from timers that have not been checked against the road.
- 2026-11-24: The capture stream carries no latitude or longitude. The repository is public and the logs may be shared; speed, heading and accuracy are all the analysis needs.
