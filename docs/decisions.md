# Decisions

One line of reasoning per decision, dated. Newest at the bottom. Hardware and architecture decisions made in chat before the repo existed are summarised in CLAUDE.md; this file starts at the repo.

- 2026-10-14: Hardware locked (4.3C, NEO-M9N SMA, ISM330DHCX, VEML7700, Qwiic). Reasons in CLAUDE.md section 2.
- 2026-10-23: OBD input (vLinker BM+, BLE, BMW UDS) brought into the first release instead of a later phase; bring-up stays staged because the dongle has not arrived.
- 2026-10-24: Dovetail flipped: rail on the shell, groove in the plate. A dovetail groove in a face that is vertical in print has a lip that starts in mid-air and cannot print; a rail with its wide end on the bed can.
- 2026-10-24: Rail leans back by RAIL_LEAN_DEG = 10 (nominal dash tilt) so the plate groove is vertical in print; the shell no longer changes when the tilt is measured, the plate absorbs it.
- 2026-10-24: Printability is checked layer by layer (check_overhangs.py, 45-degree step) before any STL is released; PrusaSlicer's warnings had been catching what a face-normal check missed.
- 2026-10-24: GPS board on 3 mm bosses, components toward the display, so its edge-mount SMA jack and plug clear the display PCB back by 1 mm. IMU moved to x = 26 to clear a connector row on the display back. Clearances taken from Waveshare's STEP, not the bare PCB outline.
- 2026-10-24: Second notch through the visor floor for the light sensor cable; the coax notch is under the antenna.
- 2026-10-24: Two Qwiic-to-header cables into the one screw terminal (IMU chain and light sensor) because the light sensor is out of 50 mm reach and the GPS board's second port faces the wall.
- 2026-10-24: M2 x 4 heat-set inserts and M2 x 6 screws for all sensor mounts: the CE10192 kit has no M2.5 and M3 inserts do not fit a 6 mm boss.
- 2026-10-24: USB-C opening is a notch from the shell's seam edge rather than a window, removing a 16 mm bridge.
- 2026-10-24: Plate adhesive is genuine 3M VHB 5952, not a general-purpose mounting tape: the F56 dash top is a grained low-surface-energy plastic (electrical tape would not stick), Gorilla's data sheet excludes polypropylene, and 5952 is the grade phone-mount makers use on dashes. Promoter only if a hidden test patch fails, because promoter makes removal leave a mark.
- 2026-11-05: Draft 2.6 shell. Both floor notches moved BEHIND the bezel pocket wall (z -22 to -13.5): draft 2.5 had them in front of it, where the 13 mm bezel wall blocks the tray, so no cable could reach the visor (found by Logan on the printed shell).
- 2026-11-05: GPS board moved to the right half and rotated 180 degrees (SMA jack pointing left) because the antenna lead has a 30 mm stiff section next to the plug: plug plus stiff lead need 50 mm dead straight from the jack, which now runs along y = 4 under bare PCB. Coax notch widened to 10 mm so plug and stiff section can be fed through at an angle; the left antenna rib stops at z -3 so the lead can leave the pocket at its front corner. IMU moved to the upper left, above that lane.
- 2026-11-05: Display press bosses replaced by four 5 mm pads on bare PCB, placed from Waveshare's STEP. The old bosses at the PCB holes seated on the DC terminal and corner standoffs and let the display sit 2 mm off-centre in the shell; the bezel pocket centres the glass, the pads only press.
- 2026-11-05: Light sensor held by M2 x 12 screws down through counterbored holes in the visor roof with nuts under the board, not inserts: there is no iron access to a boss hanging inside the visor, and a screw head on the roof looks better than a nut (Logan's call).
- 2026-11-05: GPS bosses 2 mm with the kit's M2 x 3 inserts (was 3 mm, M2 x 4) to buy clearance for the plug under the display back. SMA knockout is now a recess leaving a 0.4 mm skin, drilled only if the external puck is ever needed.
- 2026-11-08: Datum error found from Logan's photos of the 2.6 shell with only the display fitted (bezel 2 mm proud): the model took device z = 0 as the glass front, but the glass sits behind the 2 mm bezel plate, so every display-back part was 2 mm deeper than modelled and the press pads stopped 2 mm short. Fixed with PCB_BACK_Z = -(BEZ_T + STACK) = -12.2.
- 2026-11-08: Draft 2.7 (same day, withdrawn): tried to keep the 25 mm shell by running the SMA plug beside the display board; it assumed the tray was 97 mm tall inside when it is 80.3, so there is no such band. Never released.
- 2026-11-08: Draft 2.8: shell deepened by the same 2 mm (DEPTH 27) so the 2.6 layout, already checked against the display back, is unchanged relative to it; the 7.9 mm SMA plug keeps 1.5 mm under the PCB. Device back lands 12 mm from the windscreen edge instead of 14. Pad test coupon added so the datum is proven before the next shell print.
- 2026-11-08: GPS board screws are M2 x 4 (a 6 mm screw bottoms in the 3.5 mm hole); IMU stays on M2 x 6.
- 2026-11-08: Pad coupon printed by Logan: three pads reached the PCB, the fourth at (36, -21) sat on the GT911 chip on the touch flex, which folds behind the PCB and is not in Waveshare's STEP. Pad moved to (44.5, -20); the flex footprint is now in the drawings' part list.
- 2026-11-09: Logan's square-on back photo shows the STEP omits the touch flex and chip, the speaker, the SPK and battery connectors, the coin cell holder, the 6-pin JST and a PCB notch, and that its 'DC terminal' is the BOOT/RESET pair. Two coupon prints had landed STEP-placed pads on parts. Rule from now: place nothing from the STEP alone; the photo (in hardware/datasheets) is the reference.
- 2026-11-09: Draft 2.9. Press pads moved onto the four corner screw heads (certain positions, load into the panel frame). DEPTH 27 -> 31 so the plug lane keeps 5.5 mm under the PCB and clears the optocouplers and the speaker's edge. Coax notch replaced by a 6 mm slot open to the visor front: the plug and 50 mm rigid section cannot be threaded through any hole in the top wall, so the flexible lead is laid in from the front.
