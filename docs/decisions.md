# Decisions

One line of reasoning per decision, dated. Newest at the bottom. Hardware and architecture decisions made in chat before the repo existed are summarised in CLAUDE.md; this file starts at the repo.

- 2026-10-14: Hardware locked (4.3C, NEO-M9N SMA, ISM330DHCX, VEML7700, Qwiic). Reasons in CLAUDE.md section 2.
- 2026-10-23: OBD input (vLinker BM+, BLE, BMW UDS) brought into the first release instead of a later phase; bring-up stays staged because the dongle has not arrived.
- 2026-10-24: Dovetail flipped: rail on the shell, groove in the plate. A dovetail groove in a face that is vertical in print has a lip that starts in mid-air and cannot print; a rail with its wide end on the bed can.
- 2026-10-24: Rail leans back by RAIL_LEAN_DEG = 10 (nominal dash tilt) so the plate groove is vertical in print; the shell no longer changes when the tilt is measured, the plate absorbs it.
- 2026-10-24: Printability is checked layer by layer (check_overhangs.py, 45-degree step) before any STL is released; PrusaSlicer's warnings had been catching what a face-normal check missed.
- 2026-10-24: GPS board on 3 mm bosses, components toward the display, so its edge-mount SMA jack and plug clear the display PCB back by 1 mm. IMU moved to x = 26 to clear a connector row on the display back. Clearances taken from Waveshare's STEP, not the bare PCB outline.
- 2026-10-24: Second notch through the visor floor for the light sensor cable; the coax notch is under the antenna.
- 2026-10-24: Two Qwiic-to-header cables into the one screw terminal (IMU chain and light sensor) because the light sensor is out of 50 mm reach and the GPS board's second port faces the wall.
- 2026-10-24: M2 x 4 heat-set inserts and M2 x 6 screws for all sensor mounts: the CE10192 kit has no M2.5 and M3 inserts do not fit a 6 mm boss.
- 2026-10-24: USB-C opening is a notch from the shell's seam edge rather than a window, removing a 16 mm bridge.
- 2026-10-24: Plate adhesive is genuine 3M VHB 5952, not a general-purpose mounting tape: the F56 dash top is a grained low-surface-energy plastic (electrical tape would not stick), Gorilla's data sheet excludes polypropylene, and 5952 is the grade phone-mount makers use on dashes. Promoter only if a hidden test patch fails, because promoter makes removal leave a mark.
- 2026-10-24: Firmware toolchain is PlatformIO on the pioarduino platform release 55.03.311 (Arduino core 3.3.11, the version Waveshare's ci.json pins); the official PlatformIO espressif32 platform stopped at Arduino core 2.x.
- 2026-10-24: PlatformIO runs from a virtualenv on Homebrew python@3.12 (`~/.platformio/penv`, `pio` symlinked into /opt/homebrew/bin) because the pioarduino build scripts refuse Python 3.15, which is what Homebrew's own platformio formula ships.
- 2026-10-24: Waveshare's Arduino io_extension driver was ported onto Wire and the one I2cBus mutex instead of ESP-IDF's i2c_master API, so the SparkFun and Adafruit sensor libraries (which use Wire) and the board controller share one bus driver and one lock.
- 2026-10-24: The IO expander's PWM register is inverted (0 = brightest, Waveshare caps the dim end at 95 percent so the panel never blanks). IoExpander::setBacklightPercent takes human brightness and hides the inversion.
- 2026-10-24: Expected I2C map gains 0x40 (ES7210 microphone ADC, Waveshare es7210_adc.h 0x80 in 8-bit form) and confirms 0x18 for the ES8311 codec (es8311_codec.h 0x30 in 8-bit form). Both are on the bus whether we use audio or not, so the scan must expect them.
- 2026-10-24: Phase 3a drives the RGB panel with esp_lcd_panel_rgb directly, copied from Waveshare's rgb_lcd_port (IDF 5.5 renames psram_trans_align to dma_burst_size). Whether phase 3b uses ESP32_Display_Panel as CLAUDE.md section 5 says, or Waveshare's own esp_lcd_touch GT911 port, is still to decide.
- 2026-10-24: UI merged from the v2 brief and the telemetry design handoff (docs/design/handoff): six tabs Drive, Trip, Dynamics, Energy, Battery, Charge from the handoff replace Dash, EV, G detail, Timers, Trip, Settings; swipe stays as a second way to change tab; Settings by header tap. The merged prototype docs/design/mini-dash-ui.html is the visual reference.
- 2026-10-24: The handoff's demo tag in the header becomes the brief's status cluster (GPS, OBD, day or night, clock); its marketing taglines go, the space carries live data.
- 2026-10-24: 100-0 braking (time, with distance) is a Trip headline tile; max braking G moves to Journey totals and the Dynamics peaks row, because the braking timer was a committed brief feature and the G peak is still shown twice.
- 2026-10-24: The brief's EV page items find homes: torque, pedal and motor temperatures on Dynamics; current, cell voltages, 12 V on Battery; range and 12 V on Drive.
- 2026-10-24: Power bar centred on zero with an asymmetric scale (60 kW regen left in cyan, 150 kW draw right in lime), not the handoff's left-anchored segments, so regen stays visible and direction does not rely on colour.
- 2026-10-24: Energy chart plots signed battery kW over 10 minutes, not rolling Wh/km, which is undefined at low speed; Wh/km is the trip figure and only shown instantaneously above 15 km/h.
- 2026-10-24: Type scale raised for the 0.117 mm pixel: speed 88 px, tiles 40, footer values 26, labels 16, parked-only text 14. Only numerals of 40 px and up are glanceable at about 75 cm.
- 2026-10-24: Dark palette always; night mode is backlight PWM through the expander, driven by the VEML7700 (the handoff assumed no light sensor). The light palette is a later experiment for sun on the windscreen.
- 2026-10-24: Splash at power-on: typographic MINI / COOPER SE with a bottom status line, on within 300 ms, over to Drive after 1.5 to 3 s. Doubles as the acquiring / connecting state. Logan may supply artwork to replace the wordmark.
- 2026-10-24: Trip history, event traces and charge sessions go on a LittleFS partition on the internal flash (9 MB already reserved by the partition scheme), bounded to the last 60 trips; all-time records and settings stay in NVS; still nothing on the TF card.
- 2026-10-24: Font Inter with tabular digits converted for LVGL, because Montserrat lacks the dashes, dots, arrows and degree sign the design uses and its digits are not tabular.
- 2026-10-24: A labelled demo replay mode is built first in phase 3b, isolated from records, so the UI can be developed and reviewed on the bench without driving.
- 2026-10-24: The device is switched off whenever the car is parked or charging (powered only from the car's USB-C, no battery, no sleep mode). So a trip is one power cycle, checkpointed every 30 s, and the Charge tab is dropped: five tabs of 160 px, Drive, Trip, Dynamics, Energy, Battery.
- 2026-10-24: Splash artwork is the MINI wings emblem Logan supplied, lifted off its white background by a flood fill from the corners (the emblem has a continuous dark outline, so the white ring inside survives), stored in docs/design/assets at 420 px wide with transparency.
- 2026-10-24: Display pipeline: LVGL partial rendering into a 40-row internal-RAM buffer, chunks copied into the back PSRAM framebuffer, swap on the last chunk, dirty rows synced to the new back buffer. Direct mode into PSRAM cost about 150 ms a frame of per-pixel blending; this costs about 55 ms. No tearing either way.
- 2026-10-24: The G plot keeps only the static layer and the density cloud on a canvas (redrawn at most once a second); trail, vector, dot and peak markers are LVGL objects, so a tick invalidates small areas rather than the whole plot.
- 2026-10-24: Labels whose text changes every tick are fixed-width and right-aligned instead of re-aligned each tick; re-aligning invalidated them every frame even when nothing changed.
- 2026-10-24: LVGL's hot draw code goes in IRAM (LV_ATTRIBUTE_FAST_MEM = IRAM_ATTR); internal heap after boot is about 94 KB, watched.
- 2026-10-24: Inter's tabular digits are made the default digits by rewriting the font's cmap through the tnum GSUB lookups with fontTools, because lv_font_conv does not apply OpenType features.
- 2026-10-24: The UI tick is 10 Hz; frames follow the tick. The handoff's 30 fps target waits on cheaper rendering of anti-aliased text and lines.
- 2026-10-24: Demo replay is on by default while phase 3b is under way (bench use); it is labelled DEMO in the header and resets the trip when switched.
- 2026-10-24: UI text is positioned by baseline from the font metrics, not by box top. The converted digit-only faces have no leading, so box-top placement collided the speed and SOC figures with their captions (Logan's photo); a baseline grid per page also fixes the uneven alignment between mixed sizes.
- 2026-10-24: Settings gets a gear icon at the right of the header (Montserrat's built-in symbol) because the header tap was undiscoverable; the whole header remains the tap target.
- 2026-10-24: Hardware behaviour is only logged as a pass when a person or an instrument saw it; the touch row had been marked from read statistics alone and was wrong.
- 2026-10-24: GPS I2C transactions are 128 bytes (the ESP32 Wire buffer) and the receiver's UBX-only, nav-rate and autoPVT settings are checked by their acknowledgements and retried; the 80 to 108 ms bus holds went away with this.
- 2026-10-24: The I2C mutex stays non-recursive and now rejects and logs a nested take from its own holder, because a nested take silently cost 50 ms per GPS fix and halved the IMU rate.
- 2026-10-24: Storage layout: NVS for settings, mounting reference, lifetime records and the trip id counter; LittleFS on the "ffat" partition for /trips/<id>.trp (last 60), /events/<id>-<rec>.evt (12 s at 10 Hz, 10 bytes a sample) and /active.trp (checkpoint every 30 s). Files are written to a temporary name and renamed so a power cut leaves the old or the new file, never a torn one.
- 2026-10-24: Demo trips are never stored and never touch today or lifetime records (the engine runs isolated while the demo is on); their event traces are deleted when the demo trip ends.
- 2026-10-24: Session record comparisons are against the last stored trip; a first-ever record says so. Today is seeded from today's stored trips at trip start, lifetime from NVS.
- 2026-10-24: Wall clock from the board's PCF85063A RTC, set from GPS once per boot; UTC stored, Australia/Melbourne with the DST rule for display and day grouping.
