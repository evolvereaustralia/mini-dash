# Assembly

Order follows the exploded drawing `hardware/enclosure/mini-dash-layout-4-exploded.png`; the tray and visor drawings show where everything sits. Draft 2.8 layout (2.6 layout, shell 2 mm deeper): GPS board in the right half with its SMA jack pointing left, IMU upper left, antenna lead and light sensor cable through two notches in the top wall behind the bezel.

0. Before printing the shell, print  (about half an hour): drop the display onto its four pads and check the glass front is flush with the four corner posts. Proud means the pads are too tall, recessed too short; tell Claude the amount.
1. Heat-set the eight inserts (from the CE10192 kit): four M2 x 3 (3.5 mm OD) in the 2 mm GPS bosses and four M2 x 4 in the 5 mm IMU bosses. Print `export/mini-dash-insert-coupon.stl` first (10 minutes) and set two inserts in it; its holes are 3.1, 3.2, 3.3 and 3.4 mm (notches on the edge count up from 3.1) so you can confirm 3.2 is right for your printer before touching the shell (3.2 was a snug fit on 24 Oct 2026). Method: soldering iron at 230 to 250 C for PETG (an M2 insert tip if you have one, otherwise a plain conical tip resting in the insert's bore), insert sitting on the hole with its narrower, less-knurled end down, let the iron's heat sink it under its own weight plus light pressure, keep it square, stop when the top is flush with the boss, lift the iron straight out and leave it to cool untouched for a minute. On the GPS bosses do not push past flush: there is only 0.5 mm of back wall under the hole. The light-sensor bosses take no inserts (step 5).
2. Antenna first, while the tray is empty: peel the 3M pad and press the antenna onto the visor floor against the back wall between the side ribs, coax exit (under the LNA can) at the front. Lead the cable out of the pocket at its front-left corner (the left rib stops short there), back along the floor to the 10 mm coax notch at x = -21, and feed the SMA plug and the 30 mm stiff section down through it at an angle into the tray. Inside, the plug and stiff section must lie dead straight along the tray, 4 mm above the centre line, pointing right; the flexible lead bends up and left of x = -42. Note the flexible length you have before fitting the GPS board: from the antenna to the start of the stiff section needs about 85 mm.
3. GPS board onto its 2 mm bosses in the right half, components toward the display, SMA jack pointing LEFT (toward the IMU). Connect the SMA plug. Plug the 50 mm Qwiic cable into J4 (the port on the left edge, next to the jack) before the screws go in; J2 on the right edge faces the wall and stays empty. Four M2 x 4 screws (not longer: the holes are 3.5 mm deep).
4. IMU onto its 5 mm bosses, upper left, components toward the display, connector edge along X. 50 mm cable into its RIGHT port, header cable A into its LEFT port (its plug points at the left wall with 5 mm to spare). The stiff antenna lead passes beneath the IMU board, between its two bosses. Four M2 x 6 screws.
5. Light sensor: hold the board against the ends of its two bosses under the visor roof, component side down, lens forward. Two M2 x 12 button-head screws go down through the counterbored holes in the roof top, through the bosses and the board; hold an M2 nut against the board underside with needle-nose pliers and tighten gently (the board is 1.6 mm, do not crush it). The heads sit in the roof. Header cable B comes up through the 8 mm Qwiic notch at x = 22 into one of its STEMMA ports.
6. Header cables A and B into the bottom-entry screw terminal block: black GND, red 3V3, blue SDA, yellow SCL, two wires per contact. Check VOUT is 3.3 V with a meter before this step (see test-log.md). Cable A runs from the terminal down and round the left side of the tray to the IMU; cable B runs right and up over the GPS board to the Qwiic notch.
7. Display into the bezel pocket from behind. Shell over the bezel from behind until all four fingers click. The display is now trapped between the bezel lip and the four press pads (on bare PCB, so it sits flat and the bezel centres it).
8. Visor cover slides in from the right along the roof and floor grooves until it stops.
9. Plate onto the dash with VHB, groove open end to the left. Slide the device on from the left until the rail hits the groove end.

To stow: slide the device left off the plate.
