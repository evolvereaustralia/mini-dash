# Assembly

Order follows the exploded drawing `hardware/enclosure/mini-dash-layout-4-exploded.png`; the tray and visor drawings show where everything sits. Draft 2.7 layout: GPS board in the left half with its SMA jack pointing right along the top of the tray, IMU on the right under the TF slot with its ports facing up and down, antenna lead and light sensor cable through two notches in the top wall behind the bezel.

1. Heat-set the eight M2 x 3 inserts (3.5 mm OD, from the CE10192 kit): four in the 2 mm GPS bosses (left) and four in the 2.5 mm IMU bosses (right). Print `export/mini-dash-insert-coupon.stl` first (10 minutes) and set two inserts in it; its holes are 3.1, 3.2, 3.3 and 3.4 mm (notches on the edge count up from 3.1) so you can confirm 3.2 is right for your printer before touching the shell (3.2 was a snug fit on 24 Oct 2026). Method: soldering iron at 230 to 250 C for PETG (an M2 insert tip if you have one, otherwise a plain conical tip resting in the insert's bore), insert sitting on the hole with its narrower, less-knurled end down, let the iron's heat sink it under its own weight plus light pressure, keep it square, stop when the top is flush with the boss, lift the iron straight out and leave it to cool untouched for a minute. Do not push past flush on any of them: there is only 0.5 mm of back wall under each hole. The light-sensor bosses take no inserts (step 5).
2. Antenna first, while the tray is empty: peel the 3M pad and press the antenna onto the visor floor against the back wall between the side ribs, coax exit (under the LNA can) at the front. Lead the cable out of the pocket at its front-right corner (the right rib stops short there), back along the floor to the 10 mm coax notch at x = 21, and feed the SMA plug and the 30 mm stiff section down through it at an angle into the tray. Inside, the plug and stiff section lie dead straight along the top of the tray, in the 13 mm band between the display board's top edge and the top wall, pointing left toward the GPS jack; the flexible lead only has to cover about 20 mm from the notch to the end of the stiff section.
3. GPS board onto its 2 mm bosses in the left half, components toward the display, SMA jack pointing RIGHT. Connect the SMA plug. Four M2 x 4 screws (not longer: the holes are 3.5 mm deep). J4 on the right edge takes the Qwiic cable to the IMU; J2 on the left edge faces the wall and stays empty.
4. IMU onto its 2.5 mm bosses on the right, components toward the display, Qwiic ports facing UP and DOWN. Top port: 50 mm Qwiic cable to GPS J4 (48 mm needed; if it will not reach without strain, use a 100 mm cable from the elechawk kit). Bottom port: header cable A. Four M2 x 4 screws.
5. Light sensor: hold the board against the ends of its two bosses under the visor roof, component side down, lens forward. Two M2 x 12 button-head screws go down through the counterbored holes in the roof top, through the bosses and the board; hold an M2 nut against the board underside with needle-nose pliers and tighten gently (the board is 1.6 mm, do not crush it). The heads sit in the roof. Header cable B comes up through the 8 mm Qwiic notch at x = 40 into its right-hand STEMMA port.
6. Header cables A and B into the bottom-entry screw terminal block: black GND, red 3V3, blue SDA, yellow SCL, two wires per contact. Check VOUT is 3.3 V with a meter before this step (see test-log.md). Cable A runs from the terminal right along the bottom of the tray and up into the IMU's bottom port; cable B runs right along the bottom, up the right side between the display board and the wall, then left along the top band to the Qwiic notch at x = 40.
7. Display into the bezel pocket from behind. Shell over the bezel from behind until all four fingers click. The display is now trapped between the bezel lip and the four press pads (on bare PCB, so it sits flat and the bezel centres it).
8. Visor cover slides in from the right along the roof and floor grooves until it stops.
9. Plate onto the dash with VHB, groove open end to the left. Slide the device on from the left until the rail hits the groove end.

To stow: slide the device left off the plate.
